# Revenue

- Enterprise tier grew twelve percent year over year, driven by three new logos in the retail segment and two renewals that expanded their seats
- Headcount stayed flat at forty-two while the support queue doubled, which the new routing rules absorbed without a change in response time
- Hiring resumes in the first quarter with two engineers and one designer, all three already identified through the referral program
- The platform migration finished a week early and under the budget, with the last customer moved on the final Friday of the quarter
- Churn fell to the lowest level in two years after the pricing change, and the exit interviews name no single cause for the remaining departures
- The partner channel brought nine percent of new revenue, up from four, with most of it through the two agencies signed in the spring
- Marketing spend held steady while the cost per lead dropped by a third, which the team attributes to the webinar series
- Next quarter the plan is to ship the reporting overhaul, open the second region, and close the three enterprise deals in the pipeline
